Selected projects

Design system for a public-sector HR platform

2025–2026 · one of two designers

  • An audit of 24 components of a ready-made library, after which the client chose to build its own system. The header with navigation and most of the basic components are mine; with the second designer I wrote usage rules for the button: when to use it, anatomy, hierarchy, tone and placement. Developers already build the product with it: they get the variables as a file exported from Figma and keep their own component library in code, built to match the system. In development, release in November 2026.

UXdevice, a marketplace for robots

2026, three months · the only designer, freelance

  • 16 pages in four screen sizes, from desktop to phone, and all graphics, including the logo; a design system with three levels of tokens: 188 primitive, 229 semantic and 414 component tokens; a review of the studio’s build with 36 issues in three rounds. In the catalog, I argued for one list with a “Show more” button instead of pagination, and the client agreed. Launched.

TON Walk, a walk-to-earn game inside Telegram

2025, three months · the only product designer

  • A survey of 63 people, a test of a clickable prototype in three groups, 94 screens designed within Telegram’s limits (its header, the screen size, animations), 46 component sets. The interface texts are in English, and part of the work with the product team was in English. MVP released.

Miningx100, an online store for mining hardware

  • More than 60 screens, including the catalog, comparison and request flows; six landing pages; a mining profit calculator from desktop to phone; a design system. I checked the developers’ build and commented on 20 screens. Launched.
