About

I am a product designer. I have worked in design since 2022, first as a freelancer, then in product teams and studios. I studied design at IT HUB, a private college. Most of my work is design systems, UI kits and interfaces for B2B products.
Since August 2025 I have worked full-time at Mish, a design studio. Before that I worked at the studio O.dev on a walk-to-earn game and an internal finance platform. Earlier I worked for one employer on two products: SidePay, a B2B platform for project balances, and CoinUp, a mobile product with mini games. I also take project work: a robotics marketplace and a mining hardware store are two recent projects.

How I work

I start from the constraints. On UXdevice we dropped the animated concept to keep the three-month deadline. I keep tokens in order: in the same project, semantic and component tokens point only to other tokens, never to a raw value. When a studio builds my layouts, I review the build and write each issue as a card. I have run research with people on two projects: a survey of 63 people and a prototype test on TON Walk, and a usability test with 22 people for a real estate agency.
I use AI tools and say where: three of the five cases have a section “How I used AI”.

Location and languages

I live in Vienna, Austria, and I am open to product design roles in Europe.
