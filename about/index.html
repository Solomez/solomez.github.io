About

I am a product designer. I have worked in design for more than four years, since 2022: first as a freelancer, then in product teams and studios. I studied design at IT HUB, a private college. Most of my work is design systems, UI kits and interfaces for B2B products.

Since August 2025 I have worked full-time at Mish, a design studio. My main project there is a full redesign of a public-sector HR platform for civil servants, which had no mobile version: the news feed, the workplace with its calendar, vacations and documents, employee profiles, the staff directory and org structure, learning plans and the knowledge base. I built its design system with a second designer. It is in development, release in November 2026.

Before that I worked at the studio O.dev (Ошка on LinkedIn) on a walk-to-earn game and an internal finance platform. Earlier I worked for one employer on two products: SidePay, a B2B platform, and CoinUp, a mobile product with mini games. Alongside my main job, I also did project work until mid-2026: a robotics marketplace and a mining hardware store.

How I work

On UXdevice, a robotics marketplace with a three-month deadline, the client chose a plain site over my animated concept because it was faster to build, and I put the saved time into the UX. With design tokens, each value is defined once and one change reaches every screen; on UXdevice, role and component tokens only refer to other tokens, never to a fixed value. When developers build my designs, I check the result against the layouts and file each mismatch as a ticket: what is wrong, how it should look, and why. At Raduga Light and on an internal finance platform at O.dev, the design systems were already there, and I extended them. My research with users so far covers two projects: a survey of 63 people and a prototype test on TON Walk, a walk-to-earn game in Telegram; and a usability test with 22 people for a real estate agency’s website.

I use Claude and Grok for research, first drafts of components, simple layouts that describe a flow’s logic, interface copy, and checks of my flows. I design the final screens and make the decisions myself. Where I used AI, the case says what it did.
