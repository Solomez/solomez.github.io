About

I am a product designer. I have worked in design since 2022, first as a freelancer, then in product teams and studios. I studied design at IT HUB, a private college. Most of my work is design systems, UI kits and interfaces for B2B products.

Since August 2025 I have worked full-time at Mish, a design studio. My main project there is a full redesign of a public-sector HR platform for civil servants: every page of the platform and its design system, with a second designer. It is in active development and due for release in November 2026.

Before that I worked at the studio O.dev on a walk-to-earn game and an internal finance platform. Earlier I worked for one employer on two products: SidePay, a B2B platform, and CoinUp, a mobile product with mini games. I also take project work: a robotics marketplace and a mining hardware store are two recent projects.

How I work

I start from the constraints. On UXdevice, a robotics marketplace with a three-month deadline, the client chose a plain site over my animated concept because it was faster to build, and I put the saved time into the UX. I set up design tokens so that each value is defined once and one change reaches every screen. On UXdevice, role and component tokens only refer to other tokens, never to a fixed value. When developers build my designs, I check the result against the layouts and file each mismatch as a ticket: what is wrong, how it should look, and why. I also work inside design systems I did not start: at Raduga Light and on an internal finance platform at O.dev, I extended the existing systems. I have run research with users on two projects: a survey of 63 people and a prototype test on TON Walk, a walk-to-earn game in Telegram; and a usability test with 22 people for a real estate agency’s website.

I use Claude and Grok for research, first drafts of components and interface copy, and checks of my flows. I design the screens and make the decisions myself. Where I used AI, the case says what it did.
